That door would lead Pugh into the cartoonish arenas of Team Fortress 2 , which in turn introduced him to the Source engine mapping community. Eventually, this brought Pugh in contact with fellow modder Davey Wreden, with whom he would write and design The Stanley Parable —2013's gleeful poke at player agency, and a remake of Wreden's mod of the same name. Pugh is currently working on his "next big game" with Crows Crows Crows, as well acting as producer on upcoming PS5 exclusive Hunter's Moon , which he describes as a "historical folk horror game". But he took a break from all that to guide me along the yellow adventure line running through his Steam library. I must have a few hundred hours in Overwatch.

None of the other hero shooters kind of work for me. A lot of the useless rubbish, they've done a good job of slicing that out. Maybe they won't completely screw it up when they get to monetisation on it.

Everybody feels like they're working at it and discovering it. It's really fun to play in a duo lane with a friend. Me and my friend group are working our way through Big Walk , obviously.

It's interesting—and no hate on anybody involved, I love it, I love every game developer, but as a PC gamer, I saw the Big Walk announce trailer something like two years ago at some games [event], then Peak came out a suspicious amount of time later, and I'll admit my first [thought] with Peak was like, "Man, this is fucking Big Walk at home, but shipped a year faster." The initial premise of Big Walk was, it's local, play it with your friends, it's got spatial audio and you're exploring a world. And Peak just seemed to ape that in…

They didn't get spooked by everything moving ahead … it has gameplay that seeps into the way you are talking to each other, and it's really beautiful. I won a Saxxy award that I still have in my Steam inventory. It's a replacement for every class's base melee weapon.

I've served my time, and I'm sorry to all the people who played normally and got hats the normal way. If it makes anything better, I've never got an unusual hat, despite my 1500 hours and super-limited Saxxy award. It's what got me properly into mapping, which is what got me properly into working on The Stanley Parable.

Loads of people felt this way, and like I said with Worms, this kind of just levelled it up. It's like, 'Oh, it's a whole team of people working together.' You could speak online. I was part of a community server that did trial runs for maps.
