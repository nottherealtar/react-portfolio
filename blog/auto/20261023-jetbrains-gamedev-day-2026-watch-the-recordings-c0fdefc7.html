Missed JetBrains GameDev Day 2026, or want to revisit your favorite sessions? All the recordings are now available on YouTube! On September 28, we brought together game developers, engineers, and industry experts for a full day of technical talks, live demos, and discussions about the tools and challenges shaping modern game development.

From Epic’s open-source Lore VCS and ECS in a live game to Unreal Engine profiling, Unity UI automation, AI-driven PCG in Godot, and debugging across C# and C++, this year’s sessions stayed close to real production problems. Whether you joined us online, attended one of our watch parties in Belgrade or Limassol, or couldn’t make it this time, you can now catch up on everything at your own pace.
