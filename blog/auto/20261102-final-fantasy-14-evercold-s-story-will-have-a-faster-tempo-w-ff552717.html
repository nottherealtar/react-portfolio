Final Fantasy 14: Evercold's story will have a faster tempo, with worldbuilding in side quests, says Yoshi-P—but I'm not sure that was the problem with Dawntrail

"This time we are putting in serious consideration on the sense of tempo, back at Dawntrail we were being really careful in depicting new lands, new cultures, new encounters and this greatly slowed down the pacing on story progression. However, I don't think the issue in Dawntrail was its over-emphasis on worldbuilding, it was a lack of interest in how that worldbuilding connected to character.
