AI on Your Terms: How We See AI in YouTrack

With YouTrack, you choose whether and how to use AI, while we also make it easier to work with the tools you already trust. Coming soon: Ask YouTrack, an agentic AI chat that understands your work. Delegating task management to coding agents is becoming a common practice, and for such teams, YouTrack evolves from automating individual processes to serving as a shared “agentic database” for agent and human-led tasks.

When we published our YouTrack roadmap at the beginning of the year, we only briefly touched on our plans for AI. Now it’s time to share more details about how we see its role within YouTrack. Most project management tools now ship with AI built in and switched on by default.

Some teams run coding agents against their backlog all day. Others have strict policies and keep AI out completely. In this post, we explore the three options YouTrack gives you: connect your existing tools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), use the built-in YouTrack AI Assistant, or keep AI switched off.

With any of these approaches, you get the same projects, Knowledge Base, and Helpdesk on Server or Cloud. This makes YouTrack an affordable alternative to the Atlassian stack and other AI-first solutions. With YouTrack, it remains your choice Atlassian, monday.com, ClickUp, and other project management vendors are steadily expanding the role of AI in their products.

Many of the teams we talk to aren’t completely ready for this shift, and their reasons vary. For some, it’s the effect on pricing; for others, it’s features they don’t need or unanswered questions about how their data is used in AI services. When we discuss migration from Atlassian with customers, their main concerns are the announced end of Data Center support and Atlassian’s updated data contribution practices .

Effective August 17, 2026, de-identified and aggregated customer data may be used to improve Atlassian’s AI experiences, with opt-out options varying by plan. If you switch it on, there are three ways to use it: Connect your preferred AI chats, coding agents, automation platforms, IDEs, and workplace apps through YouTrack’s remote MCP server . Enable our built-in AI Assistant to turn selected text into tasks, summarize content, get reply suggestions, improve your writing, and more.

Search in YouTrack with prompts, get answers, create tasks, and propose updates – all within the chat and based on your project context. If you keep AI switched off, you still get YouTrack’s complete project management functionality, with nothing hidden behind AI features. The remote MCP server gives compatible tools access to the current YouTrack context while respecting each user’s existing permissions.

AI chats – ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and others Search and summarize tasks, support tickets, and Knowledge Base articles, or create and update work directly from an AI conversation. For example, you can easily connect YouTrack to Claude and search your project context using whatever details you have. If you’re a QA engineer managing multiplatform testing tasks, you can ask Claude to find unresolved tasks for testing on desktop, review them, and create a related task for similar testing on mobile devices in the same chat.

Coding agents and IDEs – PyCharm, IntelliJ IDEA, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and others Give agents the YouTrack context they need, so they can find related documentation and reflect development progress in tasks, all without leaving your coding environment. For example, when implementing a new product feature, developers can ask Claude Code directly in IntelliJ IDEA to create the subtasks they need to tackle next.
