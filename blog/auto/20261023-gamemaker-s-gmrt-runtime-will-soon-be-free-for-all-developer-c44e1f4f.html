GameMaker's GMRT runtime will soon be free for all developers

GameMaker's GMRT runtime will be free to use for all developers starting in 2027. In a statement announcing the news, a company spokesperson said this change is part of the company's strategy to make indie game development on GameMaker "more accessible." "Developers will have more flexibility in how they work with GameMaker and a simplified commercial model for their new projects," they stated.
