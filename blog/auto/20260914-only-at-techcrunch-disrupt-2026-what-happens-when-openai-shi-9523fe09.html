For AI founders, the biggest competitive threat may no longer be another startup. Every major release from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google raises the same question: What happens if the feature you’ve spent the last year building suddenly becomes part of the platform? It’s a question reshaping product strategy, fundraising, and company valuations across the AI ecosystem.

Today, they’re competing with foundation model companies introducing entirely new capabilities every few months. That changes almost every strategic decision — from what products to build and where to differentiate to how to raise capital and create lasting value. The conversation moves from “Can we build it?” to “Can we still own it?” That’s the challenge at the heart of “ What Happens When OpenAI Ships Your Roadmap ,” a Builders Stage session featuring leaders Michel Tricot (Airbyte), Rob Toews (Radical Ventures), and Linda Tong…

One of the biggest strategic risks facing AI startups today isn’t losing to another founder — it’s discovering that your competitive advantage has become someone else’s product update. Here, founders will examine why the next generation of AI winners may not be defined by the smartest models.
