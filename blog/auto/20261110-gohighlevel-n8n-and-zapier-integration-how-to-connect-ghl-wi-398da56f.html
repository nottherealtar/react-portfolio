Following n8n's docs: In the HighLevel Marketplace developer portal, go to My Apps → Create App and set the distribution type to Sub-Account . Add the scopes the node needs: locations.readonly , contacts.readonly , contacts.write , opportunities.readonly , opportunities.write , users.readonly . Add calendar and other scopes if you'll use them.

Copy the OAuth redirect URL from the n8n credential screen into the app's redirect URLs. Paste the app's Client ID and Client Secret into n8n, enter the scopes as a space-separated list, and connect.
