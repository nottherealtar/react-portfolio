Microsoft and Oracle bring agentic AI into flow of enterprise work

Our new integrations let AI agents reason across Oracle’s business-critical data—from finance and supply chain to HR and customer operations—and the work context Microsoft Work IQ understands across meetings, email, documents, and Teams.

This blog is co-authored by Arpan Shah, SVP of Oracle Databases and Muneer Mirza, VP of Multicloud at Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI). Today at Microsoft Ignite, we’re bringing agentic AI into the flow of enterprise work. Customers can access this combined intelligence through Excel, Teams, and Microsoft Copilot.

Built on Oracle AI Database@Azure , the integrations help businesses turn trusted enterprise data into insights and actions, with the choice, security, and performance mission-critical workloads demand. With Oracle AI Database@Azure, Oracle and Microsoft have helped customers modernize critical workloads to improve application performance and unlock more value from their enterprise data, by bringing databases closer to applications and connecting enterprise data with Microsoft AI. Working together, the agents synthesize their findings into a single operational conclusion for the admin. For end users: Oracle data in the flow of work Business users need insights from Oracle data, but they often need assistance from someone more technical to extract, format, and deliver it.

The new Oracle AI Database plug-in for Excel , available today, lets users connect Excel directly to Oracle Database, pull trusted data into the worksheet, and reason over it with Copilot without leaving the spreadsheet. No export, no manual preparation, no ticket to IT. Rather than another dashboard, or disconnected chatbot, this is AI in the flow of work, grounded in systems of record maintained in Oracle AI Database.
