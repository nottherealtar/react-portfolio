"On top of that, we are also adding a series of brand new unlockable TacSim rewards, such…

The patch size depends upon what platform you're playing on – Steam's is 3.6GB, Microsoft PC is 2.24GB, Epic Games Store is 3.99GB, Xbox Series is 1.39GB, and PS5 is a whopping 23.84GB – "Our work does not stop here.
